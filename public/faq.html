Frequently Asked Questions

Tonerly Quotes (tonerlyquotes.ca)

Getting started

What is Tonerly Quotes?

A mobile-friendly tool for printer and toner sales reps. Pick a printer model, see the toners, inks and drums it takes (with page yields), and build a customer quote in seconds.

How do I get access?

Sign in with your email address. New accounts need approval before they can use the tools, so if you see a waiting message, your request has been received and will be approved shortly.

Does it work on my phone?

Yes. It is designed for phones and tablets as well as desktop browsers, so you can quote from the field.

Quotes

How do I build a quote?

Open Quote Builder, add a printer line, choose the model, then pick the toners, inks or drums you want. Enter your customer name, adjust quantities and prices, and the totals update automatically.

Can I add something that isn't in the list?

Yes. You can add custom part numbers and extra line items with your own description and price, and they can be saved for next time.

Can I save quotes for a customer?

Yes. Save customers (with an optional address and notes) and keep several different quotes under the same customer. A switcher lets you move between them.

Can I add my logo and business name?

Yes. Your logo and business details appear on your quotes.

Prices and costs

Where do the prices come from?

Prices are yours. The site does not set prices for you. Enter your selling price and, if you wish, your cost for each part, and they are saved to your account.

How do I load many prices at once?

Go to My Prices and download the OEM or compatible Excel template. Fill in the Selling Price and My Cost columns (part numbers are locked), then upload it back. Both lists can be saved at the same time.

Can I delete a price list?

Yes. On My Prices you can delete an entire saved list, or clear individual prices.

Who can see my prices and costs?

Only you. They are stored against your login and are not shown to other users.

Printer data

How accurate is the printer and toner data?

We work to keep it correct and keep updating it, but part numbers, yields and compatibility can change. Always confirm before ordering or quoting. See the Terms of Service.

I found a wrong or missing part. What should I do?

Let us know the printer model and the correct part number or yield and we will update it.

How many printers are covered?

Over 1,600 models across Brother, Canon, Dell, Epson, HP, Lexmark, Samsung and Xerox. The exact count is shown on the home page.

Is this affiliated with the printer manufacturers?

No. Brand names belong to their owners and are used only to identify compatible products.

Account and privacy

Is my data private?

Your prices, customers and quotes are stored per account and are not visible to other users. See the Privacy Policy for details.

How do I delete my data or account?

You can delete customers, quotes and price lists inside the tool. To delete your whole account, contact us.

Does it cost anything?

See the Terms of Service for current pricing information.
